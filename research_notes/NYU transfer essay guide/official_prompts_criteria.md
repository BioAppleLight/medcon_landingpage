# NYU Transfer Essay Requirements and Official Evaluation Criteria (as of Oct 2026)

Note on method: www.nyu.edu now sits behind an AWS WAF bot check (HTTP 202 "challenge"), so live fetches came back empty. Official NYU page text below was taken from Internet Archive (Wayback) snapshots of nyu.edu. The most recent snapshot of the main transfer page is **April 17, 2026**. The most recent snapshot of the "Additional Program Requirements" page is **May 27, 2025**. Treat both as the latest verifiable official text. Anything NYU changed after those dates (for example for the Fall 2027 / 2026-27 cycle) could not be verified.

## 1. Current NYU transfer essay prompt, length, and changes over time

### Takeaway
NYU asks transfer applicants for one essay, written in the Common App for Transfer NYU section: "Please provide a statement that addresses your reason(s) for seeking transfer and the objectives you hope to achieve. How can NYU and the particular school, college, program, and/or area of study you are applying to support those goals?" The limit is **2,500 characters** (about 350-500 words). This single combined prompt has been reported since at least the 2024-25 cycle. Around 2019-20 NYU asked two essays: a separate "Why NYU?" (2,500 characters) and a "reasons for transferring" statement (3,000 characters). I could not view the prompt itself on an official NYU or Common App page. Every verbatim copy comes from third-party sources that agree with each other.

### Cited Findings
- Current prompt, verbatim, as reported for Fall 2026 entry (post dated Dec 21, 2025): "Please provide a statement that addresses your reason(s) for seeking transfer and the objectives you hope to achieve. How can NYU and the particular school, college, program, and/or area of study you are applying to support those goals?" The limit is 2,500 characters maximum — [Koppelman Group, "How to Transfer to NYU 2026"](https://www.koppelmangroup.com/blog/2025/12/21/how-to-transfer-to-nyu-2026)
- The same prompt and the same 2,500-character limit were reported for the 2024-25 cycle, where it is described as the only essay: "NYU only has one essay question for transfer applicants" (post dated Dec 19, 2024) — [Koppelman Group 2024-2025](https://www.koppelmangroup.com/blog/2024/12/19/how-to-transfer-to-new-york-university-nyu-2024-2025)
- College Transitions also gives the first sentence of the prompt verbatim with a 2,500-character limit — [College Transitions](https://www.collegetransitions.com/blog/nyu-transfer-acceptance-rate-requirements-application-deadline/)
- **Earlier version (circa 2019-20 cycle, two essays):** Post dated Dec 23, 2019 — [Koppelman Group 2019](https://www.koppelmangroup.com/blog/2019/12/23/how-to-transfer-to-nyu)
  - Essay 1, "Why NYU" (2,500 characters): "We would like to know more about your interest in NYU. We are particularly interested in knowing what motivated you to apply to NYU and more specifically, why you have applied or expressed interest in a particular campus, school, college, program, and/or area of study? You may be focused or undecided, or simply open to the options within NYU's global network; regardless, we want to understand - Why NYU?"
  - Essay 2 (3,000 characters): "Please provide a statement that addresses your reasons for transferring and the objectives you hope to achieve."
- A 2022 consultant post said transfer applicants "will have to write at least two essays." A Dec 2022 post said only that the application asks why you want to transfer and why NYU fits better than your current school — [Koppelman 2022 strategies](https://koppelmangroup.com/blog/2022/1/14/transfer-application-strategies-for-nyu); [Koppelman Dec 2022](https://www.koppelmangroup.com/blog/2022/12/27/transferring-to-new-york-university-nyu-2023)
- NYU's own Common Data Set 2024-2025 (item D5) lists "Essay or personal statement" among the items for transfer admission. The PDF text extraction does not show reliably whether the column is "Required of All" or "Required of Some" — [NYU CDS 2024-2025 (mirror of the NYU Office of Institutional Research file)](https://isduwmygvmdozhpvzaix.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/sources/nyu/2024-25/ee0239a577622fe3142ff8bba85a792c8047e89a6b31ee869d057eba6e5026d5.pdf)
- The official NYU transfer page (Apr 2026 snapshot) does **not** print the essay prompt. Its "Application Requirements" list covers transcripts, one recommendation, and the $85 fee, and it points applicants to the Common App for Transfer Students — [NYU Transfer Applicants (Wayback, Apr 17, 2026)](http://web.archive.org/web/20260417012207/https://www.nyu.edu/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants.html)
- An NYU student-ambassador blog post (Feb 7, 2025) lists "Personal statement and optional essay" among transfer requirements. It advises showing "not only who you are but what you can bring to the table," explaining the decision to transfer, why NYU fits, and naming specific classes, professors, or programs. This is a student's opinion, not admissions policy — [meet.nyu.edu, International Transfer 101](https://meet.nyu.edu/advice/international-transfer-101-guide/)

### Inferences
- Between about 2020 and 2024, NYU merged its "Why NYU" and "reasons for transferring" questions into one 2,500-character prompt. The combined prompt keeps the "school, college, program, and/or area of study" wording from the old Why NYU question, so it still tests fit with the specific program.
- The Common App for Transfer also has its own general personal statement. Whether NYU requires it in addition to the NYU prompt could not be confirmed from an official source. This matters because the ambassador post mentions both a "personal statement" and an "optional essay."
- 2,500 characters including spaces is about 400-450 words of typical prose.

### Gaps
- I could not view the 2026-27 (Fall 2027 / Spring 2027) prompt on Common App or NYU, because the Common App transfer portal needs a login and nyu.edu is WAF-blocked. No source reports a change for 2026-27.
- The exact cycle when the two essays were merged (2020-21 through 2023-24) is not established.
- Whether the Common App Transfer "personal statement" is required, optional, or not used by NYU is unconfirmed.

## 2. What NYU officially says it wants from transfer applicants

### Takeaway
NYU's official wording: transfer admission is "highly competitive," and evaluation "will focus primarily on your college/university grades and your high school/secondary school performance." The most competitive applicants have finished at least one full-time year of college by the time they enroll. NYU publishes no statement about essays beyond the prompt. Other requirements: the Common App for Transfer, all high school and college transcripts, one recommendation (up to three accepted), the $85 fee, optional testing, and program-specific requirements. Deadlines are **October 15 (Spring)** and **March 15 (Summer/Fall)**.

### Cited Findings
- "Admission for transfer students is highly competitive. NYU's evaluation will focus primarily on your college/university grades and your high school/secondary school performance. Our most competitive applicants have completed at least one full-time year of college by the time they enroll at NYU." — [NYU Transfer Applicants (Wayback, Apr 17, 2026)](http://web.archive.org/web/20260417012207/https://www.nyu.edu/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants.html)
  - Wording history: Sept 2023 said "at least 32 credits/points of coursework (one year)." Dec 2024 and Aug 2025 said "at least one full-time year of coursework" — [Wayback Sept 24, 2023](https://web.archive.org/web/20230924175530/https://www.nyu.edu/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants.html); [Wayback Aug 17, 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250817234537/https://www.nyu.edu/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants.html)
- Eligibility: completion of a U.S.-equivalent secondary education, plus either current or past degree-seeking enrollment at an accredited college (U.S. or nationally recognized abroad), or non-degree enrollment with 24 or more semester credits by the time of enrollment — [NYU Transfer Applicants (Apr 2026)](http://web.archive.org/web/20260417012207/https://www.nyu.edu/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants.html). CDS D4 also lists a 24-credit minimum — [NYU CDS 2024-25](https://isduwmygvmdozhpvzaix.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/sources/nyu/2024-25/ee0239a577622fe3142ff8bba85a792c8047e89a6b31ee869d057eba6e5026d5.pdf)
- Requirements (Apr 2026):
  - All secondary transcripts, or a GED, with graduation date
  - Transcripts from all colleges attended
  - "One letter of recommendation from a teacher, counselor, coach, supervisor, or anyone else in a position of authority (up to three accepted)"
  - Nonrefundable $85 fee
  - "Standardized testing is currently optional"
  - English testing if the applicant has fewer than 3 years of English-medium study
  - A possible Self-Reported Academic Record (SRAR) after submission
  - NYU "reserves the right to request a midterm report"
  - One application per term only (extra applications are withdrawn without refund)
  - Transfer housing is "limited and not guaranteed"
  - NYU Abu Dhabi and NYU Shanghai do not accept transfers

  Source: [NYU Transfer Applicants (Apr 2026)](http://web.archive.org/web/20260417012207/https://www.nyu.edu/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants.html)
- Financial aid: "Unfortunately, we are unable to award NYU scholarships to transfer applicants." Federal and state aid is available through the FAFSA — [same page](http://web.archive.org/web/20260417012207/https://www.nyu.edu/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants.html)
- Deadlines (Apr 2026 page):

  | Term | Application deadline | Decisions |
  |---|---|---|
  | Spring | October 15 | November-January |
  | Summer/Fall external transfers | March 15 | May-June |

  Applications are due at 11:59 p.m. EST. Applicants to programs with an artistic review are advised to submit the Common App one month early — [same page](http://web.archive.org/web/20260417012207/https://www.nyu.edu/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants.html). The CDS 2024-25 (D9) shows the same dates — [CDS](https://isduwmygvmdozhpvzaix.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/sources/nyu/2024-25/ee0239a577622fe3142ff8bba85a792c8047e89a6b31ee869d057eba6e5026d5.pdf)
  - **Changed:** In Sept 2023 the deadlines were Spring November 1, Fall external April 1, and Fall internal March 1. By Dec 2024 they had moved to October 15 and March 15 — [Wayback Sept 2023](https://web.archive.org/web/20230924175530/https://www.nyu.edu/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants.html); [Wayback Dec 31, 2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20241231000615/https://www.nyu.edu/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants.html). Third-party guides that still cite April 1 are out of date.
- Credits: NYU's transfer-credits page lists a maximum of 64 transfer credits, with the admitting school deciding what carries over. CDS D13/D14 lists 64 credits from two-year and four-year institutions, and D16 says 64 credits must be completed at NYU for a bachelor's degree — [NYU Transfer Credits](https://www.nyu.edu/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants/transfer-credits.html); [CDS 2024-25](https://isduwmygvmdozhpvzaix.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/sources/nyu/2024-25/ee0239a577622fe3142ff8bba85a792c8047e89a6b31ee869d057eba6e5026d5.pdf)
- NYU admissions blog post, "Thinking of Transferring to NYU? Here's What You Need to Know…" (Staff Writer, dated July 10, 2026):
  - Recommenders should know your academic skills and character, ideally from the past year or two: "Choose someone who knows you"
  - Applicants should explain any gaps in their education (time off, service, military)
  - Fall-only programs: Global Liberal Studies, Stern, and most Tisch majors
  - Spring entry is possible in CAS and most Steinhardt programs
  - A fee waiver can be requested in the application
  - Deadlines are the same: March 15 (Fall) and October 15 (Spring)
  - The post says nothing about the essay itself

  Source: [meet.nyu.edu](https://meet.nyu.edu/advice/submitting-a-transfer-application-to-nyu/)

### Inferences
- NYU's official language puts grades first: college GPA, then high school record. The essay's job is to make the academic fit and the reasons for transferring believable. It is not presented as a primary factor.
- Because NYU awards no institutional scholarships to transfers, the essay cannot affect merit aid.

### Gaps
- NYU publishes no rubric and no minimum or recommended GPA for transfers.
- No official NYU statement says how much weight the essay carries.

## 3. School-specific requirements affecting transfers

### Takeaway
NYU uses a single essay prompt, but each school has its own rules. CAS requires a declared major. Stern takes almost no external transfers: Fall only, first-years or sophomores only, with prerequisites. Tisch requires an artistic review for every program. Steinhardt music, performing arts, and Studio Art need an audition or portfolio, and Studio Art adds an artist statement. Gallatin and Global Liberal Studies have credit and timing limits. Tandon recommends calculus and science. No school-specific written supplement was found apart from portfolio artist statements and artistic review materials.

### Cited Findings
All findings in this section come from the official page as captured on May 27, 2025: [NYU Additional Program Requirements for Transfer (Wayback)](http://web.archive.org/web/20250527120243/https://www.nyu.edu/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants/additional-program-requirements.html).

- **CAS:** "You must declare a major when you apply." Transfers are not considered for the dual programs in engineering and dentistry.
- **Gallatin:**
  - Maximum of 64 transfer credits
  - No more than 31 business credits count toward the degree
  - The first full semester must be spent in New York
  - Spring transfers are accepted, but "students currently enrolled in their first semester of college are not eligible to apply to Gallatin for transfer admission"
- **Global Liberal Studies:** Fall and Spring entry, with a maximum of 32 transfer credits. Junior year is spent at a global center, so students should expect "at least three years at NYU."
- **Liberal Studies Core:** First-year applicants only.
- **Meyers Nursing:** Applicants who hold a bachelor's degree are directed to the 2nd-degree program. Applicants who already hold an RN license are not eligible for the bachelor's program.
- **SPS:** "No additional requirements."
- **Steinhardt:**
  - All programs admit for Fall
  - No Spring external transfers in Media, Culture, and Communication; Music Business; or Music Theory and Composition
  - Music and Performing Arts require an audition. NYU recommends applying two weeks before the deadline so the audition can be scheduled.
  - Studio Art requires "a portfolio with an artist statement directly to the Studio Art program"
- **Stern:**
  - "Admission for external transfer students to Stern is extremely competitive. Nearly all of our available space is given to applicants who are already students at NYU."
  - Applicants must apply for Fall only and be in their first or sophomore year
  - Prerequisites must be completed with letter grades (no pass/fail)
  - Sophomore entry: Calculus I or higher, plus Writing/Composition (1-2 semesters)
  - Junior entry: those courses plus introductory Microeconomics (blended micro/macro courses do not count), Statistics and Regression (credit depends on passing a Stern proficiency exam), and Financial Accounting
  - No AP credit for Statistics or English Language
  - Maximum of 64 transfer credits
- **Tandon:** Calculus, or at least Precalculus, is recommended. Chemistry and Physics are "preferred." Applicants with a bachelor's degree are directed to the 2nd-degree program.
- **Tisch:**
  - "requires an artistic review for all programs"
  - Usually up to 64 credits carry over
  - Spring entry: Cinema Studies and Performance Studies only
  - Fall programs and credit caps: Collaborative Arts (64), Dance (32), Drama (62), Dramatic Writing (56), Film and Television (56; "must begin their studies over the summer"), Recorded Music (48). Cinema Studies, Game Design, IMA, Performance Studies, and Photography and Imaging also take Fall transfers.

### Inferences
- The shared prompt's phrase "the particular school, college, program, and/or area of study you are applying to" works as the school-specific supplement. CAS applicants have already declared a major, so their essay should argue for that major in particular.
- For external applicants to Stern, the published constraints suggest the essay should show that the prerequisites are done and give a clear business reason. Even so, NYU itself says almost all Stern places go to internal NYU transfers.

### Gaps
- The program-requirements page could not be verified after May 2025. Program lists and credit caps may have changed for 2026-27.
- The exact text of Tisch and Steinhardt artistic-review prompts for transfers (for example, Dramatic Writing writing samples) was not gathered. These are on school-specific sites.

## 4. Statements from NYU admissions officers, blog, podcasts, or webinars about transfer essays

### Takeaway
I found no on-record NYU admissions officer statement about how transfer essays are read. NYU's official channels (the transfer page, the meet.nyu.edu blog, and virtual transfer information sessions) cover logistics and give no essay criteria. The only official framing is that evaluation is grade-focused.

### Cited Findings

  - Stay academic and forward-looking, and do not criticize your current school's social scene

## 5. Transfer acceptance rate data and context

### Takeaway
Fall 2024 (official CDS 2024-25): 10,155 applicants, 2,243 admitted (**22.1%**), 989 enrolled. Fall 2025 (third parties citing CDS 2025-26): 10,681 applicants, 4,128 admitted (**38.6%**). I could not check the 2025-26 figures against NYU's own file. Transfer rates swing a lot from year to year (about 20-41%) but stay well above the first-year rate (about 9% in 2024).

### Cited Findings
- **CDS 2024-2025, D2 (Fall 2024):**

  | | Applicants | Admitted | Enrolled |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Men | 4,566 | 786 | 631 |
  | Women | 5,589 | 1,457 | 358 |
  | Total | 10,155 | 2,243 | 989 |

  That gives a 22.1% admit rate and a yield of about 44% — [NYU CDS 2024-25](https://isduwmygvmdozhpvzaix.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/sources/nyu/2024-25/ee0239a577622fe3142ff8bba85a792c8047e89a6b31ee869d057eba6e5026d5.pdf). Note: the per-gender rows as extracted look inconsistent (men 631 enrolled of 786 admitted; women 358 of 1,457), which may be an extraction or column artifact. The totals are reliable.
- First-year comparison, same CDS year: 110,807 applicants, 10,232 admitted (9.2%), 5,666 enrolled — [Koppelman, NYU stats 2025](https://www.koppelmangroup.com/blog/2026/1/27/new-york-university-nyu-admissions-statistics-2025)
- **Fall 2025:** 10,681 applicants, 4,128 admitted, 38.6% ("NYU's transfer acceptance rate is 38.6% for the most recent cycle"), citing the NYU CDS 2025-2026 — [Collegewise](https://collegewise.com/blog/nyu-acceptance-rate). This is not verified against the primary CDS file.
- Historical figures (third-party, sources not cited): 2023, 10,573 applicants and 3,930 admitted (37%); 2022, 20.4%; 2021, 25.8%; 2020, 41%; 2019, 22% — [College Transitions](https://www.collegetransitions.com/blog/nyu-transfer-acceptance-rate-requirements-application-deadline/). Collegewise notes an inconsistency in its own page: the text says 27% for 2023 while its table says 37% — [Collegewise](https://collegewise.com/blog/nyu-acceptance-rate)
- NYU does not publish transfer admit rates by school. It states only that Stern external transfers are "extremely competitive" — [Additional Program Requirements](http://web.archive.org/web/20250527120243/https://www.nyu.edu/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/how-to-apply/transfer-applicants/additional-program-requirements.html)
- NYU publishes no average admitted-transfer GPA. College Transitions' suggestion of 3.7 or higher is the author's opinion — [College Transitions](https://www.collegetransitions.com/blog/nyu-transfer-acceptance-rate-requirements-application-deadline/)

### Inferences
- The overall transfer rate is much less selective than first-year admission, but it includes internal-school transfers and very different programs. Stern and many Tisch programs are likely far below the overall rate (inference from NYU's own language, not data).
- Year-to-year swings (22% to 38.6%) suggest the number of transfer places depends on how first-year enrollment turns out. This is inference only, with no NYU statement found.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve NYU's official CDS 2025-26 directly. The NYU Institutional Research site was WAF-blocked and the only copy found was a third-party mirror of 2024-25.
- No data was found for Fall 2026 transfer admissions.
- No breakdown by school or Spring vs Fall was found.
